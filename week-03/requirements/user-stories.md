# User stories — Smart Campus study room booking

The six retained stories are limited to the two supplied actors and six supplied use cases.

### US-01
**Story:** As a Student, I want to view which study rooms are free and when, so that I can choose a suitable time for individual or group study.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** Availability is shown for the rooms and time slots managed by the library.

### US-02
**Story:** As a Student, I want to reserve a free study room for a time slot, so that I can plan my study session.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** The selected room and requested start and end times are supplied with the booking request.

### US-03
**Story:** As a Student, I want to cancel a booking I made, so that the room can be made available again.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A student can cancel only a reservation that the student made.

### US-04
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to block or unblock a study room, so that I can take it out of service or return it to use.
**Priority:** High
**Assumption:** An administrator chooses the room whose availability status is being changed.

### US-05
**Story:** As an Administrator, I want to review room usage over a period, so that I can understand how the library rooms are being used.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** The administrator specifies the period to review.

### US-06
**Story:** As a Student, I want to receive a confirmation when my booking is made or cancelled, so that I know the result of my request.
**Priority:** Medium
**Assumption:** A confirmation is sent only after a booking or cancellation succeeds.
